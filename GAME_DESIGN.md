# Slowerr

A little tram. A town without an end. Take the scenic route.

V0 is a playable browser journey through Amberline, an original Mediterranean-inspired town: butter-yellow trams, terracotta roofs, sea-glass shutters, flowering trees, warm stone, and a turquoise canal. References guide mood and palette, not copied assets.

The tram starts rolling at a gentle pace. Set a target speed with the throttle; W / Up accelerates, S / Down brakes, Space pauses, C cycles follow/cinematic/cab cameras, H hides the interface. There is no failure state, timer, destination, or required task. A bell is optional and synthesized locally after a user gesture. Default journey seed: amberline. Seeds are shareable via the URL.

V0.2 extends the working game into three linked places: Marigold Quarter's narrow flowering streets, Willow Countryside's rolling meadows and woods, and Azure Waterfront's canal-side houses and stone bridges. Each district blends gradually into the next; the town layout, hills and planting vary with the seed rather than replaying a fixed scenery loop.

The Slow Roads influence is continuous automatic cruising, seamless scenery streaming, smooth graded travel, and the ability to look around freely. Travel remains on tram rails. Drag the scenery to orbit/look, scroll to zoom, double-click to recenter; C cycles Follow, Cinematic, Driver's seat and Free look. Follow distance and height are adjustable in Settings. Mode changes smoothly settle into the new view rather than retaining the previous orbit.

The default HUD is compact. Journey details are optional and H hides the full interface. Settings can jump to any of the three districts; share links include the seed and current route position, allowing the same location to be revisited. These are convenient exploration controls, not a progression system.

Art remains procedural 3D with an original illustrated palette: brush-textured stucco, irregular rooflines, balconies, flowering window boxes, layered rounded vegetation, warm light and blue-green distant hills. It does not reproduce a particular film or claim to replace a bespoke hand-painted asset pipeline. Stations, weather, day/night and V0.3 features are outside this milestone.
